🏛️ Direct Government Fee Payment Rules

  • Official Fees Only: We only display application fees that are verified from official government gazettes or portals (e.g. ₹200 for Driving Licence on Sarathi Parivahan; ₹0 for PM-Kisan and Ayushman Bharat).
